# Runmesh Entity Contributor License Agreement

> **Plain-language summary.** The entity keeps ownership of its contributions.
> This agreement is a license grant, not a copyright assignment. It allows the
> Runmesh project to include an authorized entity contribution in the
> source-available community edition and in future commercially licensed
> Runmesh editions. The signer must have authority to bind the entity. Read the
> full agreement before signing; it is a legal agreement and this repository
> does not state that it has been reviewed by a lawyer.

**Version:** 1.0<br>
**Project:** Runmesh, maintained through the `aloneio/runmesh` repository
(`Project`)

This entity agreement was prepared with reference to the license-grant model
of the publicly available [Harmony Contributor Agreement Template, combined
version 1.0 (2011)](https://www.harmonyagreements.org/agreements). It is a
Runmesh-specific agreement, not a claim that the Project uses an unmodified
Harmony form. It selects a copyright-and-patent license grant rather than
copyright assignment and identifies Runmesh's current and potential future
distribution models.

## 1. Definitions

**Entity** means the legal entity identified in the signature block, including
entities that directly or indirectly control, are controlled by, or are under
common control with it only to the extent that they own or control rights in a
Contribution. **Control** means the power to direct management or policies by
ownership, contract, or otherwise.

**Contribution** means original works of authorship, including code,
documentation, tests, designs, and other materials, submitted by the Entity or
an Authorized Contributor for inclusion in or documentation of the Project. It
excludes material clearly marked in writing as not intended to be a
Contribution.

**Authorized Contributor** means an employee, contractor, or other person whom
the Entity has authorized in writing to submit Contributions on its behalf.
**Submit** means communicate a Contribution through a pull request, patch,
issue attachment, repository commit, or another written or electronic channel
accepted by a Project maintainer.

## 2. Copyright license grant

The Entity retains ownership of its Contributions. To the maximum extent the
Entity owns or controls the relevant rights, the Entity grants the Project and
its successors, assigns, distributors, and sublicensees a non-exclusive,
worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, transferable, and
sublicensable license to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly
display, publicly perform, use, distribute, and otherwise exploit its
Contributions, in source or object form and in any medium.

The grant permits the Project to distribute Contributions under the current
PolyForm Noncommercial community license and under future licenses, including
commercially licensed Runmesh editions, Runmesh Business, and other Project
distributions. The Project is not required to use, distribute, or attribute a
Contribution in a particular way, subject to applicable law and any attribution
notice the Project has agreed in writing to honor.

## 3. Patent license grant

To the maximum extent the Entity owns or controls them, the Entity grants the
Project and its successors, assigns, distributors, and sublicensees a
non-exclusive, worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, transferable,
and sublicensable license under patent claims necessarily infringed by an
Entity Contribution alone or by combination of that Contribution with Project
Materials as incorporated by the Project, to make, have made, use, offer to
sell, sell, import, and otherwise distribute the Contribution and those
combinations.

This patent license terminates for the Entity with respect to a particular
Project Material if the Entity brings or voluntarily joins a patent claim
alleging that the Project Material directly infringes a patent, except for a
defensive claim, counterclaim, or cross-claim responding to a patent claim
first brought against the Entity.

## 4. Entity promises

The Entity represents that:

1. the signer has authority to bind the Entity;
2. the Entity has authorized each named Authorized Contributor to submit the
   listed Contributions;
3. the Entity owns or controls the rights needed to grant these licenses, or
   has written permission from every applicable rights holder;
4. submitting the Contributions and granting these licenses does not violate an
   agreement, duty, confidentiality obligation, or third-party right; and
5. the Entity has identified third-party material, required notices, and
   license terms included with a Contribution.

The Entity will keep the Project informed of additions or removals of
Authorized Contributors through the process in [docs/cla-setup.md](docs/cla-setup.md).
The Entity will not submit confidential information, credentials, personal data,
or material it lacks the right to contribute.

## 5. No warranty; no obligation

The Entity provides Contributions as-is, without warranties or conditions of
any kind, including title, non-infringement, merchantability, or fitness for a
particular purpose, to the extent permitted by law. The Entity is not required
to make future Contributions, and the Project is not required to accept or use
a Contribution.

## 6. Miscellaneous

This agreement is the entire agreement about its subject matter. A waiver or
failure to enforce a provision is not a waiver of another provision. If a
provision is unenforceable, it will be interpreted or replaced only as needed
to preserve its intended effect, and the remaining provisions remain in effect.
The Project may assign this agreement and the granted licenses to a successor
or entity that maintains the Project, provided that the assignee accepts these
obligations. This agreement does not grant the Entity commercial-use rights in
Runmesh; see [COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md](COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md).
