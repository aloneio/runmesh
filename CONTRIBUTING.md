# Contributing to Runmesh

Thank you for considering a contribution. Before opening a pull request, read
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), [LICENSE_HISTORY.md](LICENSE_HISTORY.md),
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md), and the repository's current
[`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

## Before you contribute

- Discuss substantial changes in an issue first, without exposing credentials,
  confidential data, or vulnerability details.
- Keep changes focused. Runtime, policy, service, and release behavior require
  their own technical review; do not mix them with legal or documentation-only
  changes.
- Run the relevant tests, typecheck, build, and license check. Document tests
  that cannot run locally.
- Do not add copied code, generated assets, dependencies, or trademarks unless
  you identify their source, license, required notices, and redistribution
  implications in the pull request.

## Rights and licensing

Do not submit material unless you have the right to do so. Submitting an issue
or pull request does not transfer your copyright and does not by itself prove
that a maintainer has authority to relicense your contribution. The repository
currently has no published contributor license agreement or developer
certificate of origin that grants inbound relicensing rights.

Before a contribution that will be merged, the maintainer and contributor must
record a written agreement covering the contribution's licensing and any
necessary rights for the repository's then-current licensing model. Until that
agreement exists, a pull request may be reviewed but must not be treated as
accepted for relicensing, commercial licensing, or redistribution under terms
the contributor has not granted. Maintain provenance records with the pull
request.

This safeguard intentionally avoids assuming that historical or future external
contributors consent to a license change. See the baseline authorship audit in
[LICENSE_HISTORY.md](LICENSE_HISTORY.md).

## Pull requests

Describe the problem, solution, tests, documentation changes, and any user
visible or compatibility impact. Update English and Chinese documentation
where the change affects a documented policy. A merge decision remains with the
maintainer and does not replace the written rights agreement described above.
