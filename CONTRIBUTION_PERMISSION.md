# Contribution permission

Runmesh welcomes personal, educational, research, public-interest, community,
and enterprise developers who want to improve the project. This document is a
narrow additional permission for preparing, validating, and submitting an
upstream contribution to the official Runmesh repository. It does not change
the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE) or replace a contributor
agreement.

## What this permission allows

Subject to the conditions below, an individual or entity may copy, run, modify,
and test Runmesh as reasonably necessary to prepare, validate, discuss, and
submit an intended contribution to the official Runmesh repository. An employee
may participate when the employee has permission from their employer to make
the contribution and grant the rights required by the applicable contributor
agreement.

This permission supports ordinary development activities such as creating a
branch, running tests, reproducing a bug, building documentation, and preparing
a pull request. It is intended to lower the barrier to community contribution
while preserving clear licensing boundaries.

## What this permission does not allow

This contribution permission does not authorize:

- production deployment or internal business operations by a commercial
  organization;
- providing the software as a service to customers;
- commercial SaaS, paid hosting, resale, white-label distribution, or commercial
  product integration; or
- operating the software long-term under the label of contribution testing.

A contribution, pull request, review, or merged change does not by itself grant
commercial-use rights. Commercial use still requires a separate written
agreement from an applicable rights holder; see
[COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md](COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md).

## Conditions and relationship to the license

Keep the required license and attribution notices. Do not submit material you
do not have the right to contribute. Before contributing work made during
employment, confirm that you and your employer have resolved any ownership,
confidentiality, open-source-policy, and patent obligations. The
[CLA-INDIVIDUAL.md](CLA-INDIVIDUAL.md) and [CLA-ENTITY.md](CLA-ENTITY.md)
describe the project's inbound license grant for accepted contributions.

This document is a project policy and contribution permission, not legal advice
and not a representation that counsel has reviewed it. If your intended use is
not clearly limited to contribution preparation and testing, ask the maintainer
for a private commercial-licensing contact before proceeding.
