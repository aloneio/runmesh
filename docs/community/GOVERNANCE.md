# Runmesh Governance

Runmesh is maintained as a source-available, noncommercial community edition.
The project welcomes bug reports, documentation, tests, design discussion, and
code contributions from individuals and organizations.

## Roles

- **Maintainers** review changes, protect the repository, publish releases when
  authorized, and steward the technical and legal direction of Runmesh.
- **Contributors** propose changes, provide context and tests, and retain
  attribution for their accepted contributions.
- **Community participants** help with issues, reproductions, documentation,
  and design feedback.

The maintainer list is represented by the repository owner and may grow as
responsibility is delegated. A contribution or review does not itself confer
maintainer authority.

## Decision process

Small fixes, documentation changes, tests, and clearly scoped maintenance may
be proposed directly in a pull request. Substantial changes should begin with
an issue or design discussion that explains the problem, alternatives, safety
impact, compatibility implications, and validation plan.

Maintainers seek technically reasoned consensus and may request additional
review. When consensus is not available, maintainers make the decision and
record the relevant reasoning in the issue or pull request. Security-sensitive
matters should use the private process in [.github/SECURITY.md](../../.github/SECURITY.md).

## Contributions and rights

The [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../.github/CONTRIBUTING.md) document contains the complete
contribution terms and process. Contribution activity does not itself grant
commercial-use rights. Commercial permissions are described in
[COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md](../legal/COMMERCIAL_LICENSE.md).

## Changes to this governance

Governance changes are proposed through a pull request and should include a
plain-language explanation of their impact on contributors and maintainers.
The current repository owner or delegated maintainers approve governance
changes.

This document is community guidance, not legal advice. Obtain qualified advice
for questions about your rights, obligations, or planned commercial use.
